# Plain-word meanings (use these when the policy does not define the term)

- **Premium** - the money you pay to keep the policy active
- **Sum insured / Limit** - the most the insurer will pay
- **Deductible / Excess** - the part you pay first before the insurer pays
- **Co-pay** - a fixed share of each claim that you always pay
- **Waiting period** - the time you must wait before some cover starts
- **Exclusion** - something the policy will not pay for
- **Sub-limit** - a smaller cap on one item inside the total limit
- **Pre-existing condition** - a health problem you had before buying the policy
- **Claim** - your request to the insurer to pay
- **Cashless** - the insurer pays the provider directly; you pay only your share
- **Reimbursement** - you pay first, then the insurer pays you back
- **Rider / Add-on** - extra cover you can add for extra premium

If the policy defines a term differently, always use the policy's meaning.
